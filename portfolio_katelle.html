Nubias — Gender bias detection in hiring

A two-module pipeline that removes gender bias from the hiring process end-to-end. The Job Offer module detects masculine-coded language in real time, scores neutrality out of 100 and generates a gender-neutral rewrite. The Applications module anonymises CVs at two levels — surface (names, pronouns) and full (gendered style words) — enabling truly blind shortlisting. Powered by a fine-tuned SLM (<1B parameters) on HuggingFace, respecting EU AI Act guidelines.
